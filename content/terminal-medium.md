The terminal was never merely an old interface waiting to be replaced. It is a medium with its own rhythm, constraints, language, and visual identity.

## Compression creates character

A command says less and does more. That compactness rewards learning and creates a sense of fluency.

```bash
find . -name "*.md" | sort
```

The interface becomes powerful not by exposing every possibility, but by letting people compose small ideas into larger actions.

## Aesthetic after utility

Monospace typography, blinking cursors, dark backgrounds, and restrained accent colors now appear far beyond software development. The visual language suggests focus, directness, and access to the machinery beneath the interface.
