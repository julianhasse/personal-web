Prompting treats AI interaction as a single transaction. Loop engineering treats it as a system.

## From request to process

A useful agent receives context, attempts work, evaluates the result, uses tools, incorporates feedback, and tries again. The quality comes from the loop rather than one magical sentence.

## The designer’s role

Designers are well prepared for this shift. We already define goals, constraints, states, exceptions, feedback, and recovery paths.

> The enemy is not AI. The enemy is unattended iteration.

The future belongs to people who can shape systems that notice when they are wrong and know what to do next.
