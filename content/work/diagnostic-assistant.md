# Diagnostic Assistant

Diagnostic Assistant supports healthcare providers as they find, select, order, and track laboratory tests within complex clinical environments.

## Context

Laboratory ordering spans multiple systems, payer requirements, clinical questions, and platform constraints. Providers need clarity without leaving the workflow where care is delivered.

## Challenge

The experience must support both common orders and complex tests while remaining usable inside embedded electronic medical record environments.

## Approach

The work combines product strategy, interaction design, design systems, clinical collaboration, rapid HTML prototyping, and exploration of AI-assisted workflows.

## Outcome

A more cohesive ordering experience designed to reduce friction, clarify decisions, and support future intelligent assistance.
