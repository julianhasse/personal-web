# The AI Canvas

The AI Canvas explores practical, cultural, and professional questions at the intersection of artificial intelligence and design.

## Context

AI tools are changing not only production speed, but the scope of what designers can research, build, test, and communicate.

## Approach

The project combines essays, tutorials, conversations, visual experiments, and podcast episodes into an independent publishing platform.

## Outcome

A growing body of work documenting an AI-first design practice while helping other designers navigate emerging tools and roles.
