### The Obituary for Degrees, Circa 2025

It’s 2025, and university degrees are starting to smell a bit... musty. Not quite extinct, but definitely sweating nervously. Why? Because AI just enrolled in every course on the planet, aced them overnight, and is now sitting in your browser waiting to teach *you*—for free.

In a world where 97% of developers use AI tools and over 25% of Google’s new code is AI-generated, it’s worth asking: why bother with a four-year education when your laptop can now mentor you like a caffeine-fueled polymath?

And let’s not sugarcoat it—degrees are expensive, slow, and (sometimes) about as relevant as learning Morse code for a job at TikTok.

**So if AI is this smart, are universities toast? Not quite. But they’re definitely not what they used to be.**

Let’s dissect this corpse together, shall we?

---

### Chapter 1: AI — The New Overachiever in the Front Row

AI doesn’t sleep, it doesn’t party, and it definitely doesn’t miss class. It just… learns. Everything. At lightning speed.

With tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity, you can:

- Learn quantum physics while you boil pasta.
- Get career advice while avoiding LinkedIn.
- Write code, debug it, and ship it before your coffee gets cold.

We now live in a golden age where a 14-year-old in their bedroom can out-code a CS graduate—simply because they’ve been coached by a tireless algorithm that knows every Stack Overflow answer ever written.

But that doesn’t mean AI is doing all the work *for* you. **It’s a sherpa, not a savior. If you don’t know how to learn, even the smartest AI becomes just another glorified calculator.**

---

### Chapter 2: 25 Prompts to Rule the Classroom

Why sit through three semesters of calculus when ChatGPT can spoon-feed you 80% of the important bits over a long weekend?

Prompts like:

- “Break down machine learning into a 30-day beginner plan.”
- “Turn the top 10 concepts in thermodynamics into pirate stories so I remember them.”
- “Create a quiz for me about harmonic functions with detailed feedback.”

It’s not just education. It’s *gamified*, *personalized*, and *weirdly addictive*.

The difference? You’re the one holding the controller. And unlike your Econ 101 professor, AI won’t judge your dumb questions or assign group projects with Kyle (who never responds to emails).

---

### Chapter 3: So Long, Ivory Tower

Once upon a time, a degree was your passport to the big leagues.

Now?

It’s more like a souvenir mug: nice to have, not strictly necessary.

Because AI just made education:

- **Cheaper:** Free or low-cost access to world-class content.
- **Faster:** Learn at 10x the pace of traditional classrooms.
- **Customized:** Your learning, your way, in your timezone.

Let’s be real: if the point of college was to learn skills and get a job, AI just crashed the party, ate all the snacks, and started DJing.

---

### Chapter 4: But Wait—Degrees Still Do *Something*

Just when we were ready to declare the university system legally dead, a few cold, hard truths snap us back:

#### 1. **Certifications Still Count**

Employers love shortcuts. A degree = someone else vouching for your competence. In fields like law, medicine, and engineering, that paper still holds serious weight.

#### 2. **Labs and Scalpel Stuff**

You can’t learn surgery on YouTube. And try building a particle accelerator in your backyard—actually, don’t.

#### 3. **Forced Boredom Builds Brilliance**

Universities make you eat your veggies. The dry stuff—like linear algebra—is often the key to deeper breakthroughs.

#### 4. **The Networking Game**

Sure, you can find a community online. But nothing beats dorm-room brainstorms, late-night hackathons, and professors who know people who know people.

**So no, universities aren’t dead. They’ve just been demoted from** ***the*** **path to** ***a*** **path.**

---

### Chapter 5: The Hybrid Human

Here’s the real cheat code:

**Combine the best of both worlds.**

- Use AI to learn fast and cheap.
- Use universities (or real-world mentors) to get certified, gain depth, and build networks.
- Create your own structure. Form masterminds, build projects, teach others.

Don’t go to school *or* drop out—design your own learning ecosystem. Become your own university, with AI as your unpaid (and tireless) faculty.

---

### Conclusion: Your Degree Is Dusty. You? Not So Much.

In this AI-powered renaissance, your biggest asset isn’t a diploma—it’s **your ability to learn, adapt, and build**.

Degrees still matter—but mostly to the systems that haven’t caught up yet. Meanwhile, the future belongs to those who can:

- Learn fast.
- Solve real problems.
- Collaborate across networks.

And most importantly:

**Keep evolving.**

So, whether you’re rocking a PhD or just mastered Premiere Pro in your pajamas—cheers. You’re part of the new class.

Graduation hat not required.

---

**TL;DR:** AI didn’t kill the university. But it definitely exposed its student loans and tenured blind spots. Welcome to the age of the self-made polymath, powered by prompts and curiosity.
