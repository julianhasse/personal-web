### The Role of Agentic AI in Healthcare

Agentic AI refers to systems capable of autonomous decision-making and task execution. In healthcare, such systems can:

- **Automate Administrative Tasks:** AI agents can handle scheduling, billing, and data entry, reducing the administrative burden on healthcare professionals.
- **Enhance Patient Monitoring:** Continuous monitoring of patient vitals and behaviors allows for timely interventions and personalized care.
- **Facilitate Personalized Treatment Plans:** By analyzing patient data, AI agents can assist in creating tailored treatment strategies.

---

### MultiOn’s Potential Impact on Healthcare

While MultiOn is primarily designed for web automation, its underlying technology can be adapted for healthcare applications:

- **Streamlining Clinical Workflows:** MultiOn's ability to navigate and interact with web interfaces can be utilized to manage electronic health records (EHRs), schedule appointments, and order lab tests.
- **Improving Telehealth Services:** By automating routine tasks, MultiOn can free up healthcare providers to focus more on patient interaction during virtual consultations.
- **Supporting Remote Patient Care:** MultiOn can assist in monitoring patient adherence to treatment plans by sending reminders and collecting self-reported data.
