For a while now, I’ve felt the pull to carve out a space where I can share ideas beyond fleeting social media posts or polished professional work. Something more reflective, more personal—a place where thoughts can stretch out, evolve, and invite conversation. So here we are.

This Substack will be a home for the ideas that keep me up at night, the questions that intrigue me, and the creative sparks that refuse to be ignored. Whether it’s design, storytelling, artificial intelligence, arts, or the strange intersections between them, expect a mix of deep dives, casual musings, and maybe a little unpredictability.

If any of that resonates with you, I’d love for you to join the conversation. Subscribe, reply, share your thoughts—let’s make this an exchange rather than a monologue.

More soon. Thanks for being here.
