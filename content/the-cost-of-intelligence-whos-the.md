## What’s the Intelligence Index?

The **Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index** is a composite metric launched in February 2025 to answer one question:

> *How do we objectively compare how smart large language models (LLMs) are?*

### It blends scores from:

- **AIME 2024** (Math)
- **GPQA** (Science)
- **LiveCodeBench v5** (Code generation)
- **MMLU-Pro** (Reasoning & logic)
- **Humanity’s Last Exam** (Fact + logic fusion)
- **SciCode**, **MATH-500**, and more

Each model is rated out of 100, based on aggregate performance. Think of it as an **IQ test for machines**, but cross-disciplinary.

## What Do These Models Cost?

Pricing is shown in **cost per million tokens** (input:output in a 3:1 ratio). These are prices from either the model’s *first-party API* (like OpenAI or xAI) or *median market prices* (e.g., for Meta's models).

## IQ per Dollar: A New KPI for AI

As AI apps scale, we need better ways to compare models. One emerging metric is:

> **IQ/$** — *How much intelligence are you getting per dollar spent?*

Companies will soon optimize not just for performance, but *efficiency of cognition*.

Expect:

- **Model routers** that dynamically choose the best-value LLM per request
- **Caching layers** that minimize high-cost generations
- **Blended models** where cheap ones draft and smart ones fact-check

---

## Conclusion: Intelligence Shouldn’t Break the Bank

We’re witnessing the birth of a new AI economy—where raw power is no longer king. **Efficiency** is.

**Grok 3 Mini** shows us that elite intelligence doesn’t have to come at elite prices. For developers, startups, and even enterprise ops teams, this changes the calculus of AI integration.

The future of AI won’t just be smarter—it’ll be *cheaper, faster, and more accessible to all.*
