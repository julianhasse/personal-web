I joined Substack because publishing there was easy. I am leaving because ownership matters more.

## The platform is not the work

A publishing platform can help distribute ideas, but it should not become the permanent container for them. My essays, subscribers, identity, and archives should remain portable.

## AI is part of the studio

I use AI as I use code, design software, instruments, and recording equipment: to extend what I can explore. The final judgment remains mine.

> The question is not whether a tool participated. The question is whether the creator had something worth saying.

This independent site becomes the canonical home for the work. Other platforms can help distribute it, but they no longer own the relationship.
